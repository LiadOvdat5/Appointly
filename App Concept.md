# **Business Appointment & Discovery Platform**

_A web-first solution for easy scheduling and business discovery in Israel._

---

## **Overview**

This platform is designed for **small businesses** (barbers, nail artists, beauty salons, fitness trainers, etc.) and **customers** who want an easy, centralized way to manage appointments.  
It combines **appointment management** and **business discovery** into a single ecosystem.

---

## **Core Components**

### **1. For Businesses**

- **Sign-Up & Profile Setup**
  - Business name, logo, description
  - Services offered with duration & price
  - Working hours, holidays, and special days
- **Custom Business Page**
  - Shareable link for customers
  - Light customization (colors, banner, images)
- **Appointment Management**
  - Calendar view
  - Accept/reject bookings
  - Reschedule and cancel options
- **Notifications**
  - New appointment alerts
  - Reminders for upcoming appointments
- **Analytics (Later Phase)**
  - Bookings per week/month
  - Customer retention stats

---

### **2. For Customers**

- **No Login Needed to View Pages**
  - Can see business info, services, and availability
- **Login to Book**
  - Name, phone/email
- **Personal Dashboard**
  - Upcoming appointments
  - History of past visits
- **Search & Discovery**
  - Search by category (barber, nails, beauty, massage)
  - Filter by location, availability, rating
- **Follow Businesses**
  - Quick access to favorite businesses
- **Multi-Business Calendar**
  - All appointments in one place

---

## **User Roles**

- **Business Owner** → Manages a business profile and appointments
- **Customer/User** → Books appointments and follows businesses

---

## **Key Features That Make It Stand Out**

1. **Business Ecosystem** – Not just a scheduling tool; customers can discover new businesses.
2. **No App Install Required** – Start as a responsive web app with shareable links.
3. **Simple UX for Both Sides** – Easy onboarding for small businesses.
4. **Smart Availability Search** – Customers can search for the **next available slot across multiple businesses**.
5. **Notifications** – Email, SMS, or push notifications for both parties.

---

## **Monetization Ideas**

- **Free Plan**: Basic scheduling + business page.
- **Premium Plan**: Advanced customization, analytics, featured placement in search.
- **Transaction Fee**: Take a small fee per booking (Stripe/PayPal integration).

## **High-Level Flow**

1. **Business Signs Up** → Creates profile & services → Gets a shareable link.
2. **Customer Clicks Link or Finds Business** → Views page (no login) → Logs in to book.
3. **Booking Confirmation** → Both parties get notified → Business can manage it in dashboard.
4. **Customer Dashboard** → Shows all appointments, favorite businesses, and recommendations.
