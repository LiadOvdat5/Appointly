# F-16-B: Accessibility Menu Widget

**Epic:** [[EPIC-16-Accessibility|EPIC-16: Accessibility]]
**Status:** 🔲 Not Started

---

## Overview
A persistent, floating accessibility menu (triggered by a visible button in the app header/corner) that lets users adjust the app's visual and motion settings. Preferences are saved to `localStorage` and applied on every visit.
